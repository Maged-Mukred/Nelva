## 1. Approved Strategic Foundation

### Primary positioning

> International market development for technology manufacturers.

Nelva is not positioned as an engineering contractor, sales agency, payment
software provider, certification laboratory, or complete systems integrator.
Its differentiation comes from relevant product understanding, international
B2B market-development experience, manufacturer representation, and the
ability to coordinate technical and commercial discussions between different
market participants.

### Primary audiences

1. Payment and self-service technology manufacturers
2. Other technology manufacturers with relevant international objectives
3. Distributors and system integrators engaging Nelva as clients
4. Distributors, system integrators, payment service providers, and other
   organisations being assessed as potential customers, partners, or channels
   under a manufacturer engagement

The website must always distinguish between a distributor or system integrator
acting as a Nelva client and one being developed as a potential channel for a
manufacturer.

### Approved sitemap

- Home — `/`
- Company — `/company`
- Capabilities — `/capabilities`
- Industries — `/industries`
- Contact — `/contact`

No additional pages, blog, news, insights, resources, case studies, or
placeholder sections are included in this version.

### Approved terminology

Use:

- International market development
- Manufacturer Representation
- Technical & Commercial Coordination
- Customer and partner qualification
- Commercial relationship development
- Opportunity follow-up

Do not use Technical & Commercial Coordination to imply:

- Systems integration
- Certification
- Engineering delivery
- Independent technical testing
- Authority to bind a manufacturer

## Direct Way of Working

**Label:** Approach

objective, and current commercial situation.

qualification, relationship development, manufacturer representation,
opportunity support, or a combination of agreed activities.

## Operating Principles

**Label:** Operating principles

### Practical work guided by relevance, clarity, and continuity.

#### Relevance before volume

Focus on organisations and opportunities that are relevant to the product and
commercial objective rather than prioritising the volume of contacts.

#### Careful qualification

Assess potential customers and partners before significant time and resources
are committed.

#### Clear communication

Make technical and commercial information understandable to the different
parties involved.

#### Direct involvement

Provide clients with direct access to the person responsible for the agreed
engagement.

#### Consistent follow-up

Maintain continuity beyond the initial introduction and support agreed next
steps.

#### Accurate representation

Present Nelva's capabilities, role, experience, and authority without
overstatement.

commercial coordination. It does not present itself as a manufacturer, payment
software provider, certification laboratory, engineering contractor, or
complete systems integrator.

Manufacturer representation does not automatically include exclusivity, legal
agency authority, or authority to conclude agreements on behalf of a

# 4. Capabilities

## Hero

**Label:** Capabilities

### Connected capabilities for international technology market development.

Nelva can support selected parts of the market-development process or a broader
agreed engagement—from market assessment and target identification to
relationship development, manufacturer representation, technical and
commercial coordination, and opportunity follow-up.

## Market Assessment

**Label:** 01

### Understand the commercial context before committing resources.

Nelva examines the product, its applications, relevant customer segments,
potential commercial channels, and the market participants surrounding the
opportunity.

Market assessment may help clarify:

- The product's relevant applications
- Potential customer segments
- Suitable commercial channels
- Market participants influencing the opportunity
- Questions requiring further validation
- Realistic areas for commercial investigation

The objective is to provide a more informed basis for deciding where and how
further market-development activity should begin.

## Target Identification

**Label:** 02

### Identify the organisations that matter to the objective.

Nelva researches and identifies market participants according to the agreed
engagement. These may include:

- Potential customers
- Distributors
- System integrators
- Payment service providers
- Solution providers
- Manufacturers
- Suppliers
- Other relevant commercial organisations

This is not the sale of generic contact lists. Target identification is part of
a broader process connecting research, qualification, outreach, relationship
development, and follow-up.

## Customer and Partner Qualification

**Label:** 03

### Distinguish potential fit from general market interest.

Not every company expressing interest is a suitable customer, channel, or
commercial partner.

Potential organisations may be assessed according to:

- Product relevance
- Market position
- Customer access
- Technical context
- Commercial capability
- Decision-making role
- Commitment to developing the opportunity
- Realistic potential for further discussion

Qualification improves the quality of commercial decisions. It does not
guarantee the suitability, performance, or future commitment of any
organisation.

## Manufacturer Representation

**Label:** 04

### Represent the manufacturer's commercial interests within an agreed scope.

Manufacturer Representation is a current Nelva capability.

Nelva may support a technology manufacturer by:

- Representing its commercial interests in an agreed market
- Identifying relevant customers and commercial channels
- Identifying and assessing distributors and system integrators
- Initiating and developing commercial relationships
- Supporting communication with market participants
- Coordinating relevant technical and commercial discussions
- Maintaining follow-up
- Supporting the development of qualified opportunities

Representation is defined separately for each engagement.

It does not automatically imply exclusivity, legal agency authority, or
authority to conclude agreements or bind the manufacturer. Any such authority
must be expressly confirmed in the applicable agreement.

**CTA:** Discuss a representation requirement

## Commercial Relationship Development

**Label:** 05

### Develop relevant commercial relationships over time.

Nelva may initiate contact with qualified organisations, introduce the
commercial context, support early discussions, and maintain communication as
interest and suitability become clearer.

The objective is not simply to arrange an introduction. It is to support
relevant relationships through clear communication and appropriate follow-up.

## Technical & Commercial Coordination

**Label:** 06

### Connect product requirements with commercial discussions.

Technology opportunities often involve people with different technical,
operational, and commercial responsibilities.

Nelva may coordinate communication between manufacturers, customers,
distributors, system integrators, payment service providers, and other
relevant parties.

Depending on the agreed scope, this may include:

- Clarifying initial requirements
- Coordinating relevant product information
- Supporting discussions around product suitability
- Connecting technical questions with commercial discussions
- Coordinating documentation, samples, or initial evaluations
- Keeping the relevant parties informed of agreed next steps

Nelva does not perform complete technical integrations, official
certification, engineering delivery, or independent product testing.

## Opportunity Follow-Up

**Label:** 07

### Maintain continuity after the initial discussion.

Nelva may support follow-up relating to meetings, product information,
commercial questions, samples, initial evaluations, offers, market feedback,
and agreed next steps.

Follow-up helps maintain continuity around qualified opportunities. It does not
guarantee sales, contracts, or successful market entry.

## Services for Distributors and System Integrators

**Label:** Client context

### International commercial support for distributors and system integrators.

Distributors and system integrators may engage Nelva directly as clients.
Depending on the agreed objective, Nelva may support them with:

- International market development
- Technology sourcing
- Supplier or manufacturer identification
- Commercial relationship development
- Technical & Commercial Coordination
- International opportunity support

This client role is separate from situations in which Nelva identifies or
develops a distributor or system integrator as a potential commercial channel
for a manufacturer.

## Working Process

**Label:** How Nelva works

### From commercial context to practical next steps.

#### Assess

Understand the product, applications, objective, and relevant commercial
context.

#### Identify

Find potential customers, channels, partners, and market entry points.

#### Qualify

Assess product fit, market relevance, capability, and realistic potential.

#### Develop

Initiate communication and build relationships with relevant organisations.

#### Coordinate

Support technical and commercial discussions between the parties involved.

#### Advance

Maintain follow-up and support agreed next steps around qualified opportunities.

An engagement may include selected stages or a broader agreed scope. The exact
activities and responsibilities are defined for each assignment.
